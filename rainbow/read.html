    Studying a verse

    Click any verse. A panel opens with:

    Original words
    The Hebrew, Aramaic or Greek, word by word, with how to say it and what it means. Hover a word to underline its English words in the verse (KJV and BSB). Click it to see its dictionary meaning, its grammar, how the KJV and BSB translate it everywhere, and every verse that uses it. Tick translations or books to narrow that list, and click any verse to go there.
