What counts as an echo

A run of at least seven consecutive words, in the language the book was written in, that appears in more than one place. Shorter runs are mostly stock formulas. Phrases never run across the end of a book.

Using the page

1 · Text & Match
Choose the Hebrew OT, the Greek NT, or the Septuagint with the NT, and how strictly words must agree.
2 · Repeats by length
See how many phrases there are at each length. Click a bar for one length, drag for a range.

Words

Switch Script between the original letters and transliteration. Hover any word to see its Strong's number and meaning. In an open place, the English words that translate it are underlined too (KJV, WEB and BSB). Click a word, or “Open Strong's definition”, for the full entry in a side panel. More in the guide.

Two ways of counting

Listed phrases counts each phrase once, at its full length, exactly as listed. Every repeated run also counts the shorter stretches inside longer phrases, so a 20-word repeat adds to every length from 7 to 20. More in the guide.
